Privacy policy

Job Copilot · last updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

Job Copilot is a personal job-search assistant run by Daniel Nicoară for a small, invited group of users. Contact: nicoara.dnd@gmail.com.

What Google data we access

Only if you connect a Gmail account, Job Copilot asks Google for one permission: read-only access to that mailbox (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change, label or delete email, and it asks for nothing else in your Google account.

How we use it

  • Job Copilot looks only for replies to job applications you sent through it. Every message is first checked on the computer that runs Job Copilot. Messages that do not look job-related are skipped, and messages that look financial, medical or otherwise sensitive are excluded before anything else happens to them.
  • For a job-related message, it reads the sender, subject, date and text to work out which of your applications it answers and whether it is, for example, an invitation or a rejection.
  • Job Copilot may send the text of a job-related message to its AI provider, DeepSeek, only to perform that task. DeepSeek is based in China, so this text leaves the EU. Messages that were skipped or excluded are never sent.

What we keep

For each job-related message Job Copilot keeps the sender, subject (with numbers such as amounts removed), date, Gmail message and thread identifiers, which application it belongs to or may belong to, and short quotes from the message that explain the match. It also keeps the connected mailbox's address and a Gmail sync position. It does not store message bodies. The Google sign-in token is stored, kept secret and never shown. This data is stored on the computer that runs Job Copilot. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Sharing

We do not share Gmail data with anyone, except sending job-related message text to the AI provider named above to perform the task you asked for. We transfer data only to provide or improve the features you use, to comply with the law, or to protect security.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Job Copilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No human reads your email unless you ask us to for support or security, or the law requires it. Gmail data is never used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect Gmail in Job Copilot at any time. This revokes Job Copilot's access at Google and deletes the stored token. Stored email data stays until you ask for it to be deleted (below).
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have all stored email data deleted, email nicoara.dnd@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. A material change is announced to users before it applies.
